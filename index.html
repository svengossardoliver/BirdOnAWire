Bird on a wire: tension vs weight

A bird of weight W sits at the midpoint of a light rope. Drag θ (the angle each half of the rope makes with the horizontal) and watch how the tension responds. Sign convention: right and up positive, left and down negative.

Key thresholds

  • T exceeds W (θ < 30°)
  • T equals W (θ = 30°)
  • T is less than W (θ > 30°)
  • T exceeds W/2 (θ < 90°)
  • T equals W/2 (θ = 90°, rope vertical)
